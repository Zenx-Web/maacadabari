Filmmaking is more than just storytelling, it's about crafting captivating visuals, seamless edits and immersive soundscapes. DFM is a power-packed, short-term course that introduces you to the fundamentals of filmmaking, covering pre-production techniques, linear editing, sound editing and even clay animation. With hands-on training in industry-standard software like Photoshop, Audition, Premiere and After Effects, you will develop the skills to step into roles like cinematographer, director, producer or screenwriter.

# FAQ

## What is this course about?

DFM is a short-term course covering the fundamentals of filmmaking, including pre-production techniques, linear and non-linear editing, sound editing and clay animation.

---

## Who should join this course?

This course is designed for beginners interested in filmmaking and aspiring Cinematographers, Directors, Producers and Screenwriters.

---

## Do I need prior experience?

No. The course introduces the fundamentals of filmmaking from the ground up, starting with Fundamentals of Visual Design and Fundamentals of Filmmaking.

---

## Which software will I learn?

You will learn Adobe Photoshop, Adobe After Effects, Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition and Storyboarder.

---

## What certificate will I receive?

Students receive a MAAC Certificate on completion of the course.
