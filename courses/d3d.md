# Course Overview

D3D (Program in 3D Animation Filmmaking) is a comprehensive program that prepares students for careers in professional 3D animation production.

The course focuses on the complete animation pipeline—from concept development and modeling to animation, lighting, rendering, and post-production. Students gain practical experience using industry-standard software while building a portfolio that reflects real studio workflows.

# FAQ

## Is this course beginner friendly?

Yes. The course starts with creative and design fundamentals before moving into advanced animation.

---

## Will I learn character animation?

Yes. Character animation is one of the core components of the program.

---

## Does the course include AI?

Yes. Students are introduced to modern AI-powered creative tools alongside traditional animation workflows.

---

## Which software will I learn?

Students work with Adobe Creative Cloud, Autodesk Maya, Substance 3D Painter, Arnold Renderer, and several AI-powered creative tools.

---

## What careers can I pursue?

Graduates can work in animation studios, gaming companies, advertising agencies, production houses, and visualization firms.
