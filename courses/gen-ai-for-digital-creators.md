# FAQ

## What is this course about?

Gen AI for Digital Creators teaches students to master Graphics, Video Production, Narrative Design, UI Design, Product Ad Design, Presentation Design, Digital Comics and Digital Illustration while integrating Generative AI into their creative process.

---

## Who should join this course?

This course is designed for aspiring digital content creators, Graphic Designers, Video Producers, UI Designers, Presentation Designers, Digital Illustrators, Comic Artists and creative professionals looking to integrate AI into their workflow.

---

## Do I need prior experience?

No. The course begins with AI Foundations & Prompt Engineering Basics and an Introduction to Gen AI Tools before progressing into image, video, audio and presentation generation with Gen AI.

---

## Which software will I learn?

You will learn ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Leonardo AI, Midjourney, Sora, Runway ML, ElevenLabs, Suno AI, Descript, ComfyUI, Adobe Firefly and Canva AI Tools.

---

## What certificate will I receive?

Students receive a MAAC Certificate on completion of the course.
