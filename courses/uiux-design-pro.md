# Course Overview

UI & UX Design Pro is a professional product design program that prepares students to design intuitive, user-friendly, and visually engaging digital products.

The course covers the complete UX design process—from user research and information architecture to wireframing, interface design, prototyping, usability testing, responsive design, and developer handoff.

Students learn to design websites, mobile applications, SaaS products, dashboards, and digital platforms while building an industry-ready portfolio.

# FAQ

## Is this course suitable for beginners?

Yes. The course begins with design fundamentals before moving into professional UI/UX workflows.

---

## Do I need coding knowledge?

No. Coding is not required to start the course. Basic front-end concepts are introduced to help students collaborate with developers.

---

## Which software will I learn?

Students work with Figma, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, FigJam, Miro, and other industry-standard design tools.

---

## Does this course include AI?

Yes. AI-assisted design workflows are integrated throughout the course to accelerate ideation, content creation, and design exploration.

---

## What careers can I pursue?

Graduates can work as UI Designers, UX Designers, Product Designers, Web Designers, Mobile App Designers, or freelance consultants.

---

## Will I build a portfolio?

Yes. Students complete multiple real-world projects and professional UX case studies before graduation.
