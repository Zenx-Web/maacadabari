---

# Course Overview

Compositing is the final stage of the VFX pipeline where multiple visual elements are blended into a single, believable scene.

This course focuses on advanced compositing techniques including chroma keying, rotoscoping, tracking, color matching, multi-pass compositing and cinematic finishing while building a production-ready portfolio.
