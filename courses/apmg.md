# Course Overview

The Advanced Program in Motion Graphics & Broadcast Design equips students with technical expertise, artistic creativity and AI-driven workflows for film, advertising, UI/UX and digital content creation.

Across two terms, students progress from design and digital foundations into 2D and 3D motion graphics, case-study driven production and Generative AI-powered workflows.

With hands-on training, industry-standard tools, real-world projects and portfolio development, students graduate ready for careers in Motion Graphics.

# FAQ

## What is this course about?

APMG equips students with technical expertise, artistic creativity and AI-driven workflows for film, advertising, UI/UX and digital content creation, preparing them for careers in Motion Graphics.

---

## Who should join this course?

This course is designed for students interested in Motion Graphics and Broadcast Design, aspiring UI/UX Motion Designers, Graphic Designers, Video Editors, Brand Identity Designers, and beginners seeking a career in Motion Graphics.

---

## Do I need prior experience?

No. Term I begins with Design Principles, Introduction to Digital Design and Vector Illustration before progressing into motion design and 3D motion graphics in Term II.

---

## Which software will I learn?

You will work with Adobe Express, Canva, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition, Figma, Adobe After Effects, Duik Angela, Red Giant and Cinema 4D.

---

## Does this course include AI?

Yes. Students learn Generative AI for Digital Creators across both terms, using tools such as ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Leonardo AI, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Canva AI Tools, Sora, Runway ML, ElevenLabs, Suno AI and ComfyUI.

---

## What careers can I pursue?

Graduates can pursue roles such as Motion Graphics Designer (2D/3D), UI/UX Motion Designer, Game Motion Artist, Broadcast & OTT Graphics Artist, Corporate & Explainer Video Designer, Graphic Designer, Brand Identity Designer and Video Editor.

---

## What certificate will I receive?

Students receive a MAAC Certificate upon completion of the program.
