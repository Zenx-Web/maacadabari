# Course Overview

The Professional Program in Video Production is an industry-driven program that equips students with creative, technical and AI-powered skills for filmmaking, video editing, motion graphics and content creation.

Across two terms, students build a foundation in visual design and filmmaking before mastering editing, colour grading, podcast production, social media video editing and AI-powered video and audio workflows.

Students graduate ready for high-demand careers in film, advertising, social media and digital content.

# FAQ

## What is this course about?

PPVP is an industry-driven program that equips students with creative, technical and AI-powered skills for filmmaking, video editing, motion graphics and content creation, covering visual storytelling, editing fundamentals, social media content creation and post-production mastery.

---

## Who should join this course?

This course is designed for aspiring Video Editors, Motion Graphics Artists, Video Content Creators, Podcast Producers, Social Media Content Creators, Independent Filmmakers, career switchers and beginners interested in Video Production.

---

## Do I need prior experience?

No prior experience is required. Term I begins with Fundamentals of Visual Design, Design Principles and Fundamentals of Filmmaking before progressing into editing, colour grading and AI-powered production in Term II.

---

## Which software will I learn?

You will learn Adobe Express, Canva, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Storyboarder, Adobe Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Audition, Adobe After Effects, Duik Angela, Red Giant, Adobe Rush, Adobe Media Encoder, HandBrake, Riverside, OBS and Descript.

---

## Does this course include AI?

Yes. Students learn Generative AI for Digital Content and Digital Creators across both terms, using tools such as ChatGPT, DeepSeek, Leonardo AI, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly, Canva AI Tools, Sora, Runway ML, ElevenLabs, Suno AI and ComfyUI.

---

## What certificate will I receive?

Students receive a MAAC Certificate on completion of the course.
