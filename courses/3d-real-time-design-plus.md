# Course Overview

3D & Real-Time Design Plus is designed for students who want to master modern 3D production workflows and real-time visualization technologies.

The program focuses on creating high-quality 3D assets, environments, animations, and interactive experiences using industry-standard software and game engines. Students learn both traditional production pipelines and modern real-time workflows used in games, virtual production, architecture, product visualization, and immersive media.

# FAQ

## What is Real-Time Design?

Real-Time Design focuses on creating interactive 3D content that renders instantly using engines like Unreal Engine.

---

## Is this course suitable for beginners?

Yes. The course begins with design fundamentals before moving into advanced production techniques.

---

## Will I learn Unreal Engine?

Yes. Unreal Engine forms an important part of the real-time production workflow.

---

## Does this course include AI?

Yes. Students are introduced to AI-assisted creative workflows for ideation, concept generation, and productivity.

---

## What careers can I pursue?

Graduates can work in animation, gaming, architecture visualization, product visualization, virtual production, and immersive media.
