# Course Overview

From digital filmmaking and motion graphics to lighting, rendering and editing with FCP, Broadcast Plus covers every essential skill needed to bring stories to life on TV, digital platforms and beyond.

Across three terms, students progress from visual design and filmmaking fundamentals into 3D design, lighting and rendering, and finally into 3D motion graphics, look development and colour grading.

Students build a professional broadcast design portfolio while training on industry-standard software.

# FAQ

## What is this course about?

Broadcast Plus covers digital filmmaking, motion graphics, 3D design, lighting, rendering and editing, equipping students with the creative and technical expertise to thrive in the broadcast design industry.

---

## Who should join this course?

This course is designed for students interested in Broadcast Design, aspiring 2D and 3D Motion Graphics Artists, UI Designers, Video Editors, Graphic Designers and Corporate Presentation Specialists.

---

## Do I need prior experience?

No. Term I begins with Fundamentals of Visual Design and Basics of Film Making before progressing into 3D design in Term II and 3D motion graphics and colour grading in Term III.

---

## Which software will I learn?

You will work with Adobe Photoshop, Adobe After Effects, Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe Audition, Autodesk 3ds Max, V-Ray, Cinema 4D and DaVinci Resolve.

---

## Does this course include AI?

No AI tools are included in this course's curriculum as documented.

---

## What careers can I pursue?

Graduates can pursue roles such as 2D Motion Graphics Artist, 3D Motion Graphics Artist, UI Designer, Corporate Presentation Specialist, Video Editor, Graphic Designer and Broadcast Designer.

---

## What certificate will I receive?

Students receive a MAAC Certificate upon completion of the program.
