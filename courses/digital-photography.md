# FAQ

## What is this course about?

Digital Photography is a short course covering composition, lighting, framing and editing, giving students hands-on training with professional photography techniques.

---

## Who should join this course?

This course is designed for aspiring commercial photographers, visual artists, content creators and beginners interested in photography.

---

## Do I need prior experience?

No. The course begins with the Principles of Photography and camera fundamentals such as ISO, exposure, aperture and shutter speed before progressing to composition, outdoor, portrait and product photography.
