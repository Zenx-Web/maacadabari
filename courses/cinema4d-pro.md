---

# Course Overview

Cinema 4D has become one of the most popular software packages for motion graphics and broadcast animation because of its powerful MoGraph system, intuitive workflow and seamless integration with Adobe After Effects.

This course focuses on creating professional-quality motion graphics, animated products and visual content for television, social media and advertising.
