# Course Overview

APDMC PLUS is MAAC's flagship Digital Design program, created for students who want to build careers in Graphic Design, Branding, Motion Graphics, Content Creation, Web Design, Digital Marketing, and AI-powered creative production.

The course combines creative design principles with modern digital tools, Generative AI, motion design, website creation, visual communication, and portfolio development to prepare students for today's fast-growing creative industry.

Students graduate with a diverse portfolio that demonstrates their ability to solve real-world design problems across multiple digital platforms.

# FAQ

## Is APDMC PLUS suitable for beginners?

Yes. The course begins with design fundamentals before progressing into advanced digital media creation.

---

## Will I learn Graphic Design?

Yes. Graphic Design and Branding form one of the core pillars of the program.

---

## Will I build websites?

Yes. Students learn responsive web design and website publishing using modern tools.

---

## Does this course include AI?

Yes. AI-assisted design workflows are integrated throughout the learning journey.

---

## Can I work as a freelancer after this course?

Yes. CreatorX prepares students for freelancing, personal branding, and creative entrepreneurship.

---

## What careers can I pursue?

Graduates can work in advertising agencies, creative studios, startups, IT companies, marketing firms, media houses, or as independent freelancers.
