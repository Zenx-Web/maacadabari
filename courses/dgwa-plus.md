# Course Overview

DGWA PLUS is an entry-level creative program designed for students who want to begin a career in Graphic Design, Web Design, 2D Animation, Motion Graphics, and Digital Content Creation.

The course builds a strong foundation in design principles, branding, responsive web development, digital illustration, animation, and AI-assisted creative workflows.

It is ideal for students who want to become versatile digital designers capable of working across print, web, and social media platforms.

# FAQ

## Is DGWA PLUS suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. This course is specifically designed for students with little or no prior design experience.

---

## Will I learn website design?

Yes. Students learn HTML5, CSS3, Bootstrap, WordPress, and modern responsive web design techniques.

---

## Does this course include AI?

Yes. AI-powered creative tools are introduced throughout the course to enhance productivity and creativity.

---

## Can I become a freelancer after completing this course?

Yes. CreatorX provides guidance on freelancing, client management, branding, and entrepreneurship.

---

## Which careers can I pursue?

Graduates can work in graphic design studios, web design agencies, digital marketing companies, startups, media houses, or as freelance designers.
