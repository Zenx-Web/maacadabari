# Course Overview

DAFM (Program in Animation Filmmaking) is designed for aspiring artists who want to begin their journey into professional animation and digital filmmaking.

The program introduces students to the complete animation production process—from storytelling and design fundamentals to 3D animation, lighting, rendering, compositing, and final output. It builds a strong creative foundation while preparing students for entry-level roles in the animation industry.

# FAQ

## Is DAFM suitable for beginners?

Yes. It is one of the best starting points for students entering the animation industry.

---

## Do I need drawing skills?

No. Drawing skills are helpful but not mandatory. The course develops creative and technical abilities from the ground up.

---

## Will I learn 3D animation?

Yes. Students learn the fundamentals of 3D animation, modeling, lighting, and rendering.

---

## Does this course include AI?

Yes. AI-assisted creative workflows are introduced to help students improve productivity and creativity.

---

## What careers can I pursue?

Graduates can begin careers in animation studios, digital media companies, advertising agencies, and content creation.
