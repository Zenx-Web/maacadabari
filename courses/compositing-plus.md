# Course Overview

Compositing is one of the most important stages of the VFX production pipeline. It transforms multiple visual elements into a final polished shot.

This course introduces students to professional compositing workflows including keying, masking, tracking, color correction, CG integration and shot finishing while developing a production-ready portfolio.
