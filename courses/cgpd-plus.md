## Design with Purpose.

CGPD Plus is a professional course focused on graphic design, branding, print media and digital publishing. Learn how to transform creative ideas into professional marketing materials using industry-standard Adobe Creative Cloud applications.

From logos and brochures to packaging and magazine layouts, students build practical design skills while developing a portfolio suitable for advertising agencies, publishing houses and creative studios.

---

# Course Overview

Graphic design continues to be one of the most in-demand creative professions. Every business needs visually compelling branding, marketing materials and digital assets.

CGPD Plus introduces students to modern design principles, typography, layout design, branding, print production and digital publishing through hands-on projects and real-world workflows.
