# Course Overview

VFX PLUS is a comprehensive Visual Effects program designed to prepare students for professional careers in film, television, OTT platforms, advertising, gaming, and digital production.

The course introduces students to the complete VFX production workflow, including compositing, camera tracking, lighting, rendering, FX simulations, motion graphics, and AI-assisted creative workflows. Students develop practical production skills while building an industry-ready portfolio.

# FAQ

## Is VFX PLUS suitable for beginners?

Yes. The course starts with creative fundamentals before introducing professional VFX workflows.

---

## Will I learn Nuke?

Yes. Nuke is one of the primary compositing applications covered during the course.

---

## Does this course include AI?

Yes. AI-powered creative tools are introduced throughout the learning journey to improve productivity and creativity.

---

## What careers can I pursue?

Graduates can work in film production, television, OTT content creation, advertising agencies, gaming studios, and post-production houses.

---

## Will I build a portfolio?

Yes. Students graduate with practical projects and a professional demo reel suitable for job applications.
