Maya Pro is a professional training program focused on Autodesk Maya, one of the most widely used software applications in the animation, VFX and gaming industries. Learn modeling, texturing, rigging, animation, lighting and rendering while building production-ready assets.

---

# Course Overview

Autodesk Maya is the industry standard for creating characters, environments and cinematic animations used in blockbuster films, television, gaming and virtual production.

This course introduces students to the complete 3D production pipeline through practical projects and hands-on learning.
