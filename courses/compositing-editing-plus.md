# Course Overview

Compositing & Editing Plus is a professional post-production program focused on video editing, compositing, motion graphics, visual storytelling, and finishing workflows.

Students learn how to transform raw footage into polished visual content using industry-standard editing and compositing software. The course also introduces AI-assisted workflows that accelerate content creation while maintaining professional quality.

Graduates build a portfolio suitable for careers in film, television, OTT platforms, advertising, YouTube production, digital marketing, and social media content creation.

# FAQ

## Is this course suitable for beginners?

Yes. The course is designed for beginners who want to build professional editing and compositing skills.

---

## Which software will I learn?

Students learn Adobe Premiere Pro, Adobe After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, Adobe Audition, Mocha Pro, and Adobe Photoshop.

---

## Does this course include AI?

Yes. Students are introduced to AI-powered tools for editing, content generation, voice creation, visual enhancement, and creative ideation.

---

## Can I become a YouTube editor after this course?

Yes. The course prepares students for careers in YouTube production, digital marketing, social media, advertising, and post-production.

---

## Will I build a professional portfolio?

Yes. Students complete multiple real-world editing and compositing projects before graduation.
