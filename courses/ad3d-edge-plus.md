# Course Overview

AD3D EDGE PLUS is MAAC's flagship animation program designed for students who want to build professional careers in 3D Animation, Game Art, Visualization and AI-powered content creation.

The course combines creative fundamentals, production workflows, Generative AI, 3D animation, real-time technologies and portfolio development to prepare students for modern creative industries.

Students learn both traditional production techniques and AI-assisted creative workflows used in today's studios.
