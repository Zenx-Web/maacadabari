# Hero

## Create Beyond Boundaries.
### Design the Future.
#### Master AI, XR & Creative Technologies.

The International Program in Visual Arts, Design & XR (IPVAD-XR) is MAAC's most comprehensive career program, combining Digital Design, Video Production, 3D Content Creation, Visual Effects, XR Technologies and AI-powered creative workflows into one structured learning experience.

Designed for the next generation of creators, this program prepares students for studio careers, freelancing and entrepreneurship across the rapidly growing AVGC-XR industry.

---

# Course Overview

IPVAD-XR is a future-ready international program that develops multidisciplinary creative professionals capable of working across multiple industries.

Rather than focusing on a single specialization, students gain expertise in design, filmmaking, 3D, animation, VFX, XR, Unreal Engine and Generative AI while also learning business, freelancing and creator economy skills.

Graduates leave with an extensive portfolio and real-world production experience.
