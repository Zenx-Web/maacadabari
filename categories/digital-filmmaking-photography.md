# What is Digital Filmmaking & Photography?

Digital Filmmaking & Photography blends creative storytelling with technical precision, from capturing the perfect shot to crafting cinematic edits.

MAAC's programs teach students to work with professional gear and industry tools, covering the full journey from pre-production to post, so they can turn vision into visual impact.

Digital Filmmaking & Photography is used in

• Films

• Brand Shoots & Campaigns

• Digital Platforms

• Social Media Content

• Podcasts

• Photography

• Content Creation
