# What is Digital Design?

Digital Design is the process of creating visual content for digital platforms.

It combines creativity, communication, branding, marketing, technology and user experience to solve real business problems.

Students learn to design content that is attractive, functional and ready for modern digital platforms.

Digital Design is used in

• Branding

• Advertising

• Social Media

• Digital Marketing

• Web Design

• Mobile Apps

• Motion Graphics

• UI Design

• Content Creation

• E-commerce

• Product Marketing
