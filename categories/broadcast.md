# What is Broadcast Design?

Broadcast Design brings motion graphics, storytelling and visual identity together to create powerful visual experiences across television, digital streaming and live broadcasts.

MAAC's Broadcast courses equip students with in-demand skills in digital filmmaking, motion graphics, 3D design, editing and compositing, preparing them for a career where creativity meets real-time storytelling.

Broadcast Design is used in

• Newsrooms

• OTT Platforms

• Television

• Digital Streaming

• Live Broadcasts

• Media Houses

• Production Studios

• Digital Platforms

# Industry Overview

Broadcast Design sits at the centre of today's content-driven landscape.

Media houses, production studios and digital platforms rely on skilled broadcast designers to craft high-impact visuals for newsrooms, OTT platforms, television and live broadcasts.

MAAC's Broadcast curriculum blends motion graphics, 3D design, editing and compositing with AI-driven workflows to prepare students for this fast-evolving industry.
