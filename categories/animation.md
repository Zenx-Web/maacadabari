\# What is Animation



Animation is the process of creating the illusion of movement through digital characters, environments and visual storytelling.



Modern animation combines creativity, storytelling and advanced software to create immersive experiences across multiple industries.



Animation is used in



• Movies



• OTT Platforms



• Television



• Gaming



• Advertising



• Architecture



• Product Visualization



• Virtual Production



• XR Experiences
