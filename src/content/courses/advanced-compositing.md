  ## Combine.

  Advanced Compositing is designed for aspiring VFX artists who want to master professional compositing techniques used in films, television, OTT productions and commercials.   Compositing is the final stage of the VFX pipeline where multiple visual elements are blended into a single, believable scene.

  This course focuses on advanced compositing techniques including chroma keying, rotoscoping, tracking, color matching, multi-pass compositing and cinematic finishing while building a production-ready portfolio.
