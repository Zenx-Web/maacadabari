# Purpose

The Course Overview page helps students choose the right creative field before selecting a specific course.

This page should NOT display complete course information.

Instead, it introduces the major creative industries and allows visitors to navigate to the appropriate category.

Think of this page as a career selection page rather than a course listing.

# Choosing The Right Course

Help students understand the difference between categories.

Animation

Best for students who enjoy storytelling, characters, films and 3D worlds.

Visual Effects

Best for students interested in movies, simulations, compositing and cinematic production.

Digital Design

Best for students interested in branding, graphics, web design, social media and content creation.

UI / UX

Best for students interested in websites, apps and digital product design.
